The Opportunity

Where USG Water Is Today

USG Water Solutions operates across a high-touch, relationship-driven field service model — but critical revenue signals are siloed across disconnected systems. Field technicians lack real-time customer intelligence. HubSpot marketing data doesn't connect to CRM outcomes — attribution is invisible. And Warren Equity Partners has no unified, real-time view across its portfolio for board-level reporting.

Salesforce proposes a unified platform addressing all four of these gaps simultaneously — replacing fragmented tooling with a single AI-powered revenue engine purpose-built for the water treatment and utilities sector.
